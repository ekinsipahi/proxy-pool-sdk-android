# Code of conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is competent and acting in good
faith, and say the useful thing rather than the clever one.

## What that means here

**Do:** ask what someone was trying to do before telling them they did it wrong;
explain your reasoning so the next person learns something; accept that a
maintainer may say no to a change and that this is not personal.

**Don't:** demean people, derail threads, or make anyone's participation about
anything other than the work. Harassment of any kind — public or private — ends
your participation in this project.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere else the
project is being discussed.

## If something goes wrong

Email **conduct@sterr.network**. Reports are read by the maintainers, kept
private, and acted on. Consequences range from a private word to a permanent
ban, depending on what happened; we will tell the reporter what was decided.

Maintainers are held to this more strictly than anyone else, not less.
